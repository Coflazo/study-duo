Study Duo

Get Study Duo

A study timer that lives in your browser. Offline, free, no account. About two minutes.

1. Paste this in Terminal and press Return

curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Coflazo/study-duo/main/install.sh | sh

Open Terminal: press Cmd+Space, type Terminal, press Return.

2. Then three clicks in your browser, once

  1. Turn on Developer mode on the extensions page that opens.
  2. Click Load unpacked.
  3. Choose the Study Duo folder; its path is already on your clipboard.

What the line does, and nothing else

It downloads the latest release from GitHub over HTTPS, stops unless its checksum matches, unzips it into a visible folder called Study Duo in your home folder, copies that path and opens your browser's extensions page. No admin rights. You can read the script first.

To update, run the same line again, then press reload on the Study Duo card. Your data stays.
