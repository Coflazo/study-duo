Study Duo

Count the music on your phone

Study Duo runs in your computer's browser, so it can't see the apps on your phone. Your phone can send each song it plays to a free listening history instead, and Study Duo reads that history for the songs that played during your study blocks.

1. Make a free account

ListenBrainz is free and open source and needs no key. Last.fm works too, but Study Duo then needs a free API key from last.fm/api.

2. Send what your phone plays

Spotify, on any phone: in ListenBrainz, open your settings, choose Connect services and link Spotify. ListenBrainz then records what you play on Spotify by itself.

Android, any music app: install Pano Scrobbler (open source, with a free version) and sign in with ListenBrainz or Last.fm. It sends what any app plays.

iPhone, Apple Music: iPhones don't let apps see what other apps play, so use an Apple Music scrobbler for Last.fm from the App Store. Several exist; some cost money.

3. Tell Study Duo your username

  1. On your computer, open Study Duo's dashboard and go to Connections.
  2. Type your ListenBrainz username and press Connect (for Last.fm, your username and API key).

Study Duo asks that service for your own recent songs every 30 minutes and keeps only the ones that played during a study block or break. It sends nothing about you. Turn it off any time with Disconnect.
